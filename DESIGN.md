# Design direction

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## World

Modern editorial portfolio: warm paper ground, deep ink-navy text, electric-blue accents, fine rules, and a mix of practical sans-serif copy with a distinctive display serif for emphasis.

## Typography

DM Sans carries navigation, labels, body copy, and controls. DM Serif Display is reserved for short emphasis phrases and the page's human voice. Display type uses tight tracking and short line lengths.

## Color

- Paper: `#f4f1eb`
- Paper deep: `#e8e3da`
- Ink: `#10243b`
- Ink soft: `#526174`
- Electric blue: `#1666d9`
- Rule: `#c7c5bd`

## Composition

The hero is a two-column working-record index rather than a metric dashboard. Interior sections use an editorial label rail, ruled lists, and generous vertical rhythm. Experience and skills are intentionally foregrounded for recruiter scanning.

## Interaction

The only interactive motion is native smooth scrolling and a compact mobile navigation state. Focus rings remain visible, external links identify themselves with an arrow, and reduced-motion users receive instant scrolling.

## Content rules

Only the confirmed profile, experience, education, skills, contact details, and LinkedIn URL are presented as factual. Projects are explicit placeholders until source details are supplied.
