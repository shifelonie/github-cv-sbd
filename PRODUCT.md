# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams evaluating Fadhil Iman Pratomo for full-stack or web-development internships.

## Product Purpose

A single-page portfolio that helps visitors quickly understand Fadhil's capabilities, experience, education, and how to contact him.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents a junior developer's factual experience and technical range with calm, editorial clarity rather than invented metrics or claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan on mobile or desktop, often in under a minute, then follow the experience, skills, LinkedIn, or email paths.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a responsive, English-only, single-page experience with semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and no fabricated project details, employers, achievements, metrics, testimonials, certifications, or links. A phone number must not be displayed. Missing project details use explicit placeholders.

## Brand Commitments

The confirmed name is Fadhil Iman Pratomo. The visual direction is modern editorial: warm off-white, deep navy, electric-blue accents, refined typography, generous whitespace, and a technical-but-personable tone.

## Evidence on Hand

Confirmed profile, skills, employment and education details, public email address, and LinkedIn URL are supplied in the build brief. Project names, descriptions, technologies, outcomes, and URLs are not yet supplied and must remain placeholders.

## Product Principles

- Make recruiter scanning fast without flattening the person behind the work.
- Use only verifiable content.
- Let technical capability and real experience lead the story.
- Keep the interface calm, memorable, and easy to navigate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, descriptive links, keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
